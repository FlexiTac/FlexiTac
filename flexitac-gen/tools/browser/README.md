# Browser checks (Playwright)

End-to-end checks of the browser UI in `web/`, archived from the stage-F work
of the 2026-10-13/14 audit fix.  They drive headless Chromium against a
running `app.py`, so they need Playwright and are **not** part of the stdlib
unittest suite (`unittest discover -s tests` never collects `tools/`).

The page's look is `web/theme.css` (2026-11-04: Apple-style), linked once right after `web/styles.css`, which stays
unchanged: each of its rules repeats the selector of the `styles.css` rule it overrides.  It is light for every
visitor whatever the system's scheme, dark once the header's toggle is on (2026-11-06: `#themeToggle`, right before the
GitHub card on both pages; `web/theme.js`, loaded in `<head>` before the style sheets, sets `<html data-theme>` from
the choice kept in `localStorage` under `fpc-customizer.theme`, and every dark rule hangs on that attribute).  The
checks turn the toggle (`rp.set_look`) or store its choice (`rp.DARK_CHOICE`) for "dark", never the system's scheme.
The drawings' areas (the 3D view and its pills, the previews, the stacked view and, in Gen by Surface, the sensor on
the part beside it (2026-11-05: a second `.stacked-canvas`, after `#stackedPreview`), the map) stay light lightboxes
in the dark scheme.  `theme_checks.py` checks it in the browser; `tests/test_web_theme.py` reads it without one (what
it may not declare, its palette's contrast, the reduced-motion rules, the dark rules' hook and the toggle's markup).
